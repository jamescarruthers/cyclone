5EEC: Shuffle the five crates
Copied here from F88A by F74E; always called via the JP at FE92. Called once per level start (5B2C) and continuously (hundreds of times per second) from the main menu loop at E29E, so the final crate layout depends on how long the player waits in the menu. Pass 1 removes the crates placed last time: for every one of the 38 candidate cells in 5F61 whose flag is $FF, the flag is cleared and the crate tile ($8B/$57/$2F/$0F, 5F59) is turned back into the plain tile ($58/$30/$10/$04, 5F5D); a cell whose crate was already winched up already holds the plain tile and is left alone. Pass 2 picks 5 different cells: random L from 8B74 until L<$4C, index=L/2 (0-37, uniform), retry if already flagged; flag it $FF and turn the plain tile into the matching crate tile ($58->$8B, $30->$57, $10->$2F, anything else->$0F).
