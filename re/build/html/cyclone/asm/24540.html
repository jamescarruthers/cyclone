5FDC: Interrupt: crash noise
Jumped to from the interrupt handler $83C2 while the crash flag $7505 is 1 (crash sequence). Once per frame: random HL (8B74); if L < $46 there is no sound this frame (70/256), otherwise 3 square-wave cycles are played, with the high phase lasting (H AND $F0) loop passes and the low phase (L AND $F0) loop passes (~43 T-states per pass), which gives a random-pitch rasping noise. Border stays black. Exits through the interrupt handler's register restore at $842D.
