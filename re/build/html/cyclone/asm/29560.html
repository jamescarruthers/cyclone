7378: Cyclone buffeting of the controls
Called from the main loop (5B94) after the controls have been read (7FD6) when the heli-cyclone map distance 7550 is below 5 (level 1) or 6 (later levels; the CP operand at 5B93 is patched by 8321). Adds random control inputs to 7522 so the helicopter is thrown about; very close to the cyclone it forces DOWN+LEFT (a descending spin).
Input
A Cyclone distance (7550), 0..5
Random bits come from L of the RNG at $8B74 (each bit ~50%). dist 4..5: bits 1/3/4 (down, right, left) may be added; dist 2..3: bits 0/1/3/4 (also forward); dist 0..1: controls are replaced by $12 (down+left) and the turn timer is run down one extra step.
