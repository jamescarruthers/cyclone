7ADC: Sample the ground under the helicopter and draw its shadow
Called from 7E10 (7F77) while the sprite tiles are being built, unless the helicopter has crashed. 1) Copies the already-projected codes of buffer columns 10-12, rows 15..5 to 7A9F. 2) Converts the 9 raw codes at 7A81 into terrain levels (7A8B, threshold table 7AC1) and shadow row offsets (7A95). 3) Hides shadow cells that would be covered by nearer terrain. 4) Jumps to 7BB8 to draw the shadow.
Hiding rule (per column, offsets h15, h14, h13 without bit 7): the row-13 cell is hidden if h13+2 < h15, or h13+1 < h14; the row-14 cell is hidden if h14+1 < h15. On equality the bit-7 flags (tile $18) decide.
