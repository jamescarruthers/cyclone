8135: Flight controls: turning, thrust/coasting, climbing, descending
Reached by fall-through from 80AA/80F9 once per main-loop iteration. Applies the control word 7522 to the helicopter: (1) turning in steps of 2 (=45 degrees) of the 16-step heading with a 3-iteration turn timer, (2) thrust: speed 7527 rises by 1 per iteration up to 7 while forward is held and decays by 1 when not, the helicopter moving one map unit along X and/or Y per iteration while speed>0 (direction 753B is latched while thrusting, so the craft coasts in the old direction after releasing), (3) climb/descent with an acceleration of 1,2,3 pixels per iteration, altitude 750D limited to 0..60.
Input
(7522) control bits
(7515) 1 = landed (no turning or movement; climb = take off)
(7516) 1 = fuel exhausted (no thrust, no climb, forced descent)
Output
(7500),(7502) map X and Y (16-bit)
(7506) heading 0-15 (always even: 0 N, 2 NE, 4 E, 6 SE, 8 S, 10 SW, 12 W, 14 NW)
(7507) heading shown by the sprite (odd during a turn)
(750D) altitude 0-60 pixels
(7514) set to 1 when trying to descend below altitude 0
