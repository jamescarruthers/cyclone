826F: Movement opcode table
Indexed by the direction of travel d (0-15): byte d is the opcode applied to X, byte d+1 the opcode applied to Y ($00 NOP, $23 INC HL, $2B DEC HL). Only even d are used (headings are always even), giving N (0,-1), NE (+1,-1), E (+1,0), SE (+1,+1), S (0,+1), SW (-1,+1), W (-1,0), NW (-1,-1). For d=15 the Y opcode would be the 'S' at 827F (LD D,E, harmless).
