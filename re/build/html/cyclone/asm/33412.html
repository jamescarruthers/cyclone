8284: Count down the time-left bar (T)
Called once per main-loop iteration (5BD7). Every (828C) iterations ($FF=255 in round 1, 15 fewer each round) erases one pixel line from the top of the T bar (column 30, rows 1-5, 40 lines). When the pointer passes the bottom of the bar it becomes $40DE and the main loop (5BCC) jumps to 92C8: "NO TIME", game over.
Input
(7528) iteration counter
(751C) screen address of the last erased line of the T bar, i.e. the line just above the top lit one ($471E = full)
The comparison operand at 828C is patched by 8372 (reset to $FF on a new game) and 83AD (minus 15 on each completed round).
