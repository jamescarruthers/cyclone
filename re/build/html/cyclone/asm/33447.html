82A7: Burn fuel (F bar)
Jumped to from 8470 when not on the base pad (and called by 92A9 to drain the tank during the death sequence). Every 49 iterations erases one pixel line from the top of the F bar (column 28, rows 1-5, 40 lines). After the last line, 7516 is set to 1 (tank empty).
Input
(7517) fuel sub-counter
(7518) screen address of the last erased line of the F bar, i.e. just above the top lit one ($471C = full, $47BC = empty)
Output
(7516) 1 when the tank runs dry
82A7 LD HL,$7517 Sub-counter 0..48
82AA INC (HL)
82AB LD A,$30
82AD CP (HL)
82AE RET NC
82AF LD (HL),$00
82B1 LD HL,($7518) Move the pointer down one pixel line
82B4 INC H
82B5 LD A,H
82B6 CP $48
82B8 JR NZ,$82CB
82BA LD A,L Off the bottom of the bar (row 5)? Then the tank is empty
82BB CP $BC
82BD JR NZ,$82C5
82BF LD A,$01
82C1 LD ($7516),A
82C4 RET
82C5 LD H,$40 Next character row
82C7 LD A,$20
82C9 ADD A,L
82CA LD L,A
82CB LD (HL),$00 Erase the line
82CD LD ($7518),HL
82D0 RET
