8321: Start a round: crate boxes, round bonus, difficulty
Called at 5B26 at the start of every round (new game, or after the 5th crate has been delivered to the base). Blanks the five crate boxes (attributes of rows 22-23, columns 0-9) and lights them one by one with a beep and a 0.19 s pause. On a new game (7554<>5) it resets the time-bar period to 255, sets the wind threshold 5B93 to 5 and picks a random cyclone starting corner (754B/754C). After a completed round (7554=5) it awards the round bonus, resets the crate counter, sets the wind threshold to 6 and shortens the time-bar period by 15. Finally clears the lives-lost counter 9245 and redraws the HUD.
Input
(7554) crates delivered (5 = round complete)
(9245) lives lost this round (0-2)
Output
(5B93) wind threshold (operand of CP at 5B92: 7378 runs while 7550 < it)
(828C) time-bar period in iterations
(754B),(754C) cyclone start position (new game only)
