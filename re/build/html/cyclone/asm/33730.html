83C2: Interrupt handler: rotor and winch sounds, crash noise
Reached 50 times a second through the IM2 vector (FFFF->F3F3: JP 83C2). Silent when 752D=1 (menu, or after the crash sequence starts). When 7505=1 (helicopter destroyed) jumps to the explosion noise at 5FDC, which returns through 842D. Otherwise, unless landed (7515) or out of fuel (7516), plays one rotor 'chop': a 4-interrupt cycle of one short high burst (10 cycles, half-periods 18 DJNZ loops) and three lower bursts (6 cycles, half-periods 36), both half-periods shortened by 2*speed. While 7555 is non-zero (winch line moving) a short BEEPER tone (HL=50, DE=8) precedes the chop.
Input
(752D) 1 = sound off
(7505) 1 = helicopter destroyed
(7515),(7516) landed / out of fuel: no rotor sound
(7555) winch counter (non-zero = winch sound)
(755D) rotor phase 0-3
(7527) speed 0-7
