843A: Fill the time-left bar, paying 40 points per line still lit
Called at the start of every round (5B29). Walks the T bar (column 30) from row 5 line 7 up to row 1 line 0 (40 pixel lines). A line that is still lit from the previous round earns 40 points (6600) and a $FF-pitch beep; an unlit line is lit with a $D0-pitch beep. Leaves 751C = $471E (full bar) and resets the time sub-counter.
