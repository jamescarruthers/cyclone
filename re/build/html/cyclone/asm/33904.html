8470: Fuel: refuel on the base pad, otherwise burn
Called once per main-loop iteration (5BD4). When not on the base helipad (752E=0) jumps to 82A7 to burn fuel. On the pad, adds one pixel line to the F bar per iteration (with a $D0-pitch beep) until the bar is full (pointer reaches row 0, $471C), and clears the empty-tank flag 7516. A full refuel from empty takes 40 iterations (about 4 seconds).
Input
(752E) 1 = landed on the base helipad
(7518) F-bar pointer
Output
(7516) 0
