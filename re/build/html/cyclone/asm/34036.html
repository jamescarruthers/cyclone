84F4: Draw the HUD
Draws the static instrument panel: attributes for columns 24-31 of rows 0-20 (from 7200, 21 x 8 bytes) and for rows 22-23 (7148, 64 bytes); panel graphics for columns 24-31 of rows 0-14 from the tile map 7300 (15 x 8 codes, $FF = leave blank; tile n at 7400+8n): A S F T letters, bar scales, compass rose, three helicopter (lives) icons; the bottom two rows of text from 6848 (crate boxes, WIND FORCE, DANGER); BEWARE / AIRCRAFT (invisible until 91B4 colours them); the VIEW label; then falls into 8580 to print NORTH. Called at game start (5B06), every round start (83BA) and from E3C5.
