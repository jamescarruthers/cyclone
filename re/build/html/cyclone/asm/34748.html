87BC: Update and print the high score
Compares the 7-character score at 661E with the high score at 6627 (ASCII digits, leading '.' which sorts below '0'). If the score is higher it is copied over the high score. Then prints the high score right-to-left on the H line (row 20, ending at column 31) via 6F06. Called at the start of each game (5B09, before the score is cleared) and from E3EA.
