87F0: Aircraft: move, steer by schedule, warn, place in view
Called once per main-loop iteration (5B3F). (1) Every 28 iterations advances the schedule pointer 8A69 by 20 through 15 records at E740-E858 and applies the record to the 19 aircraft of the live table E86C (5 bytes each: direction code, X, Y): a byte with bit 7 set also respawns that aircraft at its start position (E8CD+5n). (2) Moves every aircraft by its direction code d (multiple of 4): the 4 bytes at E700+d are opcodes (NOP/INC BC/DEC BC) patched into 8867/8868 (X) and 8871/8872 (Y): 16 directions, 2 units per iteration on the main axis. (3) Proximity: |dx|<=22 and |dy|<=25 sets 7530=3 (BEWARE AIRCRAFT flashes, 91B4). (4) An aircraft within |dx|<=12, |dy|<=15 is placed in the view buffer as a 3x3 block of codes $F6-$FE at F71E + (dx+12) + 23*(dy+15) (mirrored for the south view); for the first such aircraft the sprite for its direction (DDB8+72*(n+1)) is copied to the aircraft tiles $3C-$44 (FBE0) and a dithered copy to the shadow tiles $33-$3B (FB98), and the tile-stack pages are updated so the shadow sits on the ground and the aircraft at height level 87EF.
Input
(7500),(7502) helicopter X, Y
(7504) view direction
(7533) helicopter height level
Output
(7530) bit 0 = aircraft near in X, bit 1 = near in X and Y (value 3 = warning)
(7524),(7525) view cell column/row of the aircraft block
(752F) 1 once an aircraft sprite has been set up this iteration
