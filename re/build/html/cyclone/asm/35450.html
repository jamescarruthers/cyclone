8A7A: Landing / terrain-collision check
Called once per main-loop iteration (5B54). Does nothing while landed (7515=1). Otherwise (a) every other group of 4 calls updates 7533, the target height level (0-7) of the helicopter's own tile column, from the altitude; (b) compares altitude/4 with the height class of each of the 9 footprint cells (7A8B, filled by 7ADC) and decides between "still flying", "touch-down" and "crash". Touch-down then requires every footprint cell's top tile to be landable; landing on 9 helipad tiles sets 752E=1. A crash is signalled by 7505=1 (main loop then jumps to the death sequence at 9246).
Input
(7515) 1 = landed (routine returns at once)
(750D) altitude in pixels (0..60)
(7514) 1 = helicopter tried to descend below altitude 0 (set by 8269)
(751B) descent speed 0..3 (pixels per iteration while 'down' held)
(7A81) 9 raw terrain codes under the helicopter (3x3, filled by 7E10)
(7A8B) 9 height classes of those cells, in units of 4 altitude pixels (filled by 7ADC)
Footprint order in 7A81/7A8B: view-buffer cells (row15,col10),(14,10),(13,10),(15,11),(14,11),(13,11),(15,12),(14,12),(13,12).
