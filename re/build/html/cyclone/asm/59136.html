E700: Aircraft movement opcodes
16 directions x 4 bytes, patched into the aircraft mover at $8867/$8868 (X) and $8871/$8872 (Y) by $87F0: $03 = INC BC, $0B = DEC BC, $00 = NOP, so each axis moves -2..+2 per iteration.
